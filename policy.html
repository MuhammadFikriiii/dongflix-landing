Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Welcome to DongFlix. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the DongFlix application and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in to DongFlix using Google OAuth, we collect the following information provided by your Google account:

  • Email Address: Used as your primary account identifier and for account recovery.
  • Name: Used to display your profile name within the application (e.g., in the comments section).
  • Profile Picture (Avatar): Used as your avatar in the application.

We do not collect any other personal information without your explicit consent.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your DongFlix account.
  • To display your profile name and avatar when you interact with the community (e.g., leaving comments).
  • To synchronize your watch history and saved lists across your devices.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your data is securely stored in our Supabase database and is only accessible by you and the application administrators for maintenance purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can request account deletion by contacting us, or by using the account deletion feature within the application (if available). Upon request, all your personal data (email, name, avatar, watch history) will be permanently deleted from our servers.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Authentication is securely handled by Supabase and Google OAuth.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at dongflix@example.com.
